About This Sector

Overview

Supporting farmers with modern solutions, market access, and vital resources. Get farming tips, connect to crop markets, and access machinery rentals.

Who Is This For?

Farmers, agricultural workers, farm equipment suppliers, and seed distributors.

Our platform is fully inclusive - whether seeking services, offering skills, or looking for opportunities, GVCDA bridges you to the right solution.

How It Works

1
Choose your service from the options below.
2
Fill in your contact details and requirements.
3
Submit via WhatsApp to connect with our agents.
What We Offer

Key Services

Crop Support

Crop planning, weather alerts, pest control and soil health guidance.

Seeds & Fertilizers

Quality seeds, fertilizers, pesticides and crop nutrition products.

Farm Equipments

Tractor rental, rotavator, sprayers, drip kits and farming machinery.

Irrigation Services

Drip irrigation, sprinkler systems, water management and bore well support.

Organic Farming

Organic farming guidance, vermicompost, bio-fertilizers and natural inputs.

Agricultural Training

Modern farming techniques, government scheme awareness and farmer training.

Market Linkage

Crop price updates, mandi connections, buyer introductions and APMC access.

Farmer Support Services

Government schemes, Kisan credit card, crop insurance and subsidy assistance.
